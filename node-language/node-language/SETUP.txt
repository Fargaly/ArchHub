ArchHub — install on your machine
=================================

1. Copy this whole folder to your computer (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click  SETUP.bat
3. When it finishes, double-click  ArchHub  on your Desktop.

That is all. If anything refuses, send Ahmed the text in the window --
it says exactly what stopped, and nothing pretends to have worked.

Needs Python 3.11 or newer (python.org, tick "Add to PATH").
